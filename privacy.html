JH Kota — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

JH Kota ("the app") is a messaging app developed by Jarif Hasan Sagor.

This policy explains what data the app handles and why, in plain language.

What data we handle

  • Account information — when you create an account, your email address,

    • display name and (optionally) phone number are stored so other users can

    find and message you. Web guests provide only a display name and are

    flagged as guest accounts.

  • Messages and media — the messages, photos and status updates you send

    • are stored on our backend so they can be delivered to the people you send

    them to and synced across your devices. Status updates are shown for

    24 hours.

  • Call data — voice and video calls are carried over encrypted WebRTC

    • connections. We keep a simple call log (who called whom, when, voice or

    video) so the app can show your call history. We do not record calls.

  • Presence — the app stores your "last seen" time and shows when you are

    • online, so your contacts know when you are reachable.

    Backend provider

    Data is stored and processed using Supabase (database, authentication,

    file storage and realtime delivery). Data is protected by row-level

    security rules: you can only access your own account data and the

    conversations you are a member of.

    What we do NOT do

  • No ads.
  • No selling of personal data — we never sell, rent or trade your data

    • to third parties.

  • No tracking for advertising — the app contains no advertising or

    • third-party analytics trackers.

  • No access to your contacts list — the app does not upload your

    • phone's address book.

    Permissions

  • Camera & microphone — only used during voice/video calls and when

    • you choose to share a photo.

  • Photos — only the images you pick to send or post as a status.

    • Your choices

  • You can edit your name and phone number in the Profile tab at any time.
  • To delete your account and associated data, contact us at the email

    • below and we will remove it.

    Children

    JH Kota is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly

    collect data from them.

    Changes

    If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at the same

    URL with a new "last updated" date.
